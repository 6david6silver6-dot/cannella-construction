About us

Local, family-run, and proud of the work

Cannella Construction is a family owned and operated business based in Turlock, California. We work closely with homeowners to design and build spaces that fit their needs, using high-quality materials and careful craftsmanship.

Whether it's a modern kitchen upgrade or a calm, functional bathroom, the goal is the same: a room you'll love for years that also adds real value to your home.
